How itworks

  

    1
    
Look at the position

    
Every position comes from a real tournament or Lichess game. Nothing is built to trick you.

  

  

    2
    
Make the call

    
White, Black, or equal. One judgment, no move to find. Close answers earn partial credit.

  

  

    3
    
Find out why

    
Stockfish gives the verdict. A written breakdown covers the key imbalance, the common mistake, and the plan the engine prefers.
