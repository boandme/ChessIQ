About ChessIQ

Positional training for real games

ChessIQ is an independent student-built chess-training project created by Boandme. It is built around a question strong players answer constantly: who is better here, and why? Rather than asking for a forcing tactical sequence, ChessIQ presents a real position and asks whether White is better, Black is better, or the position is approximately equal.

The aim is to develop a fast, reliable positional first impression by recognizing the factors that shape real games: piece activity, space, pawn structure, king safety, weak squares, open lines, initiative, material, and coordination.

How a training round works

Start with the board, assess the imbalances, and make your call without live engine assistance. After answering, ChessIQ shows the evaluation and a concise pre-generated explanation of the ideas that mattered—turning a result into reflection on which strategic clues you could have used more effectively.

  • Read the position. Identify the side with the more active pieces, safer king, better structure, or clearer plan.
  • Make a judgment. Choose White advantage, Equal, or Black advantage.
  • Review the why. Compare your intuition with the evaluation, positional themes, and strategic direction.

What ChessIQ includes

ChessIQ combines real-game positional puzzles with focused feedback and long-term training tools.

  • Adaptive PR and difficulty paths for Easy, Medium, and Hard practice, plus targeted thematic training.
  • Daily puzzles, achievements, Analytics, puzzle history, and a global leaderboard to support consistent improvement.
  • Community results and AI explanations that explain the key positional ideas behind each answer.

Chess expertise & quality review

ChessIQ is peer reviewed by an International Master and two National Masters, with feedback from experienced expert chess players. Their practical perspective helps keep the project grounded in serious chess thinking and supports a high-quality, instructive training experience.

Project, data & contributors

Real-game position data is sourced through the Lichess ecosystem. ChessIQ uses chess-engine evaluation and position-processing tools to prepare positions, organize difficulty, and support the feedback shown after each answer.

  • Testing — EntyXD
  • Icons and visual assets — UXWing
  • Image editing and selected UI design — PlumPaletteCreations

How To Play

You'll be given a position. Read it, then call who holds the advantage:

  • White Winning — choose the White tile
  • Black Winning — choose the Black tile
  • Equal Position — choose the Equal tile
