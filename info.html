About ChessIQ

Positional training for real games

ChessIQ is a chess-training application built around a question strong players answer constantly: who is better here, and why? Rather than asking you to find a forcing tactical sequence, ChessIQ presents a real position and asks whether White is better, Black is better, or the position is approximately equal.

The aim is to develop a fast, reliable positional first impression. Players learn to recognize the factors that shape real games: piece activity, space, pawn structure, king safety, weak squares, open files and diagonals, initiative, material, and overall coordination.

How a training round works

Start with the board, assess the important imbalances, and make your call. After answering, ChessIQ shows the engine evaluation and a concise explanation of the ideas that mattered. The feedback is designed to turn a result into reflection: not only whether your judgment was right, but which strategic clues you could have used more effectively.

  • Read the position. Identify the side with the more active pieces, safer king, better structure, or clearer plan.
  • Make a judgment. Choose White advantage, Equal, or Black advantage without relying on a live engine.
  • Review the why. Compare your intuition with the evaluation, positional themes, and the strategic direction of the position.

What ChessIQ includes

ChessIQ combines real-game positional puzzles with focused feedback and long-term progress tracking.

  • 900+ real positions, Adaptive Position Rating (PR), and Easy, Medium, and Hard practice paths.
  • Daily puzzles, streaks, achievements, statistics, and a global leaderboard to support consistent training.
  • Community results and pre-generated AI explanations that explain the key positional ideas behind each answer.

Quality review

ChessIQ is peer reviewed by an International Master and two National Masters. Their perspective helps keep the training experience grounded in serious chess thinking, while the app’s real-game data, engine evaluation, and educational explanations support a high-quality standard for every session.

How To Play

You'll be given a position. Read it, then call who holds the advantage:

  • White Winning — choose the White tile
  • Black Winning — choose the Black tile
  • Equal Position — choose the Equal tile
