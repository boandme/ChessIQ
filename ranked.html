How Ranked works

Two modes

Blitz Solo spins two random themes, then gives you ten positions from them. Duels put you against another player on the identical set, at the same time.

The clock

Easy 15s, Medium 22s, Hard 30s — harder positions get more room. Three seconds between puzzles so a new board never lands mid-thought.

Scoring

100 points for a correct answer, plus up to 50 for time left. Ten slow correct answers beat five fast ones. Wrong answers and timeouts score zero, never negative.

Ranked Rating

Runs 0–3,200 and is completely separate from your PR. Duels carry full weight; Solo carries a quarter and needs at least 4 of 10 correct to move anything.

Tiers & seasons

Pawn through Grandmaster. Each season keeps 70% of your rating at rollover, and the tier you held when it closed becomes a permanent badge.

No help mid-run

Explanations are locked until the end. Afterwards you get every position back in full, with the complete AI breakdown — and in a Duel, your opponent's answers beside yours.
