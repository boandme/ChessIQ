Thematic Analysis & Training

Targeted practice

Welcome to ChessIQ

Train your positional knowledge and master the art of self-analysis — a skill invaluable for chess players of every level.

How To Play

Read the position, then decide which side has the advantage.

  • White Winning — White has the better position
  • Equal — the position is approximately balanced
  • Black Winning — Black has the better position
