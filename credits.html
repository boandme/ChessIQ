Credits & Acknowledgments

Project

ChessIQ is an independent student-built chess-training project created and designed by Boandme. The app was built to make positional evaluation practice more accessible, turning real chess positions into short, reflective training rounds.

Chess review & quality

ChessIQ is peer reviewed by an International Master and two National Masters. Their feedback helps keep the project connected to practical, high-level chess thinking and supports the app’s commitment to a high-quality training experience.

Data & technology

Real-game position data is sourced through the Lichess ecosystem. ChessIQ uses chess-engine evaluation and position-processing tools to prepare puzzles, organize difficulty, and support the feedback shown after each answer.

Thank you to the open chess and developer communities whose tools, game data, and ideas make ambitious student projects possible.

Community & feedback

ChessIQ grows through the players who use it, test it, and share what would make their training better. Feedback, feature ideas, and thoughtful criticism are always welcome. You can read the project introduction and join the conversation in the Chess.com feature on ChessIQ.

Welcome to ChessIQ

Train your positional knowledge and master the art of self-analysis — a skill invaluable for chess players of every level.

How To Play

You'll be given a position. Read it, then call who holds the advantage:

  • White Winning — choose the White tile
  • Black Winning — choose the Black tile
  • Equal Position — choose the Equal tile
