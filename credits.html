01

Project

ChessIQ is an independent, student-built chess-training project created and designed by Boandme. It exists to make positional evaluation practice something you can actually sit down and do, by turning real chess positions into short, reflective training rounds.

02

Chess review and quality

ChessIQ is peer reviewed by an International Master and two National Masters, with play testing from club players rated 2000 and above. Their feedback keeps the project connected to practical, high-level chess thinking, and it is the main check on whether a position's stated evaluation and written explanation hold up.

Reviewer names are published only with permission, which is why this page names titles rather than people.

03

Data and technology

Real-game position data is sourced through the Lichess ecosystem. ChessIQ uses chess-engine evaluation and position-processing tools to prepare puzzles, organize difficulty, and support the feedback shown after each answer.

Thank you to the open chess and developer communities whose tools, game data, and ideas make ambitious student projects possible.

Community and feedback

ChessIQ grows through the players who use it, test it, and say what would make their training better. Feature ideas and thoughtful criticism are always welcome. You can read the project introduction and join the conversation in the Chess.com feature on ChessIQ.

Welcome to ChessIQ

Train your positional knowledge and master the art of self-analysis, a skill invaluable for chess players of every level.

How To Play

You'll be given a position. Read it, then call who holds the advantage:

  • White Winning: choose the White tile
  • Black Winning: choose the Black tile
  • Equal Position: choose the Equal tile
